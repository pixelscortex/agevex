`HOST_LIMITS`, exported from `agevex`, pairs each tunable policy’s default and
hard maximum. Use the `Limits` type for `registerAgent({ limits })` overrides.
Policy rows come first below; the remaining bounds are internal runtime facts.
[Per-agent run settings](/docs/concepts/runtime#per-agent-run-settings) have their
own validated defaults, hard ceilings and combined action budget.

Subjects and agent names are at most `nameLength` UTF-16 units, and every
stored function handle at most `functionHandleBytes` UTF-8 bytes (real Convex
handles are about 60–100). Sends, delegations and native writes beyond them
fail with `INVALID_SUBJECT`, `INVALID_AGENT` or `INVALID_FUNCTION_HANDLE`
before anything is saved. With every field at its cap, an assignment stays
under its 14 KiB envelope.

`slicesPerTurn` and `stepsPerTurn` also bound approval resumes, which keep the
turn's step count; see
[approvals and the step allowance](/docs/concepts/runtime#approvals-and-the-step-allowance).

Policy rows can be lowered even when their default equals their maximum;
non-policy rows are fixed. Convex permits eight scheduled slots and 30-minute
default-runtime actions; Workflow bounds its own journal and step payloads.

Lane overrides configure one shared pool pair per mount. Defaults: roots 2,
children 4; maxima 4 each, combined at most 8 slots. All registrations on a
mount must agree. Sleeping workflows consume no action slot.
